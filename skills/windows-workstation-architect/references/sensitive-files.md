# Sensitive Files

Sensitive personal files include identity scans, contracts, tax files, bank records, insurance, legal documents, recovery codes, and private keys.

Guidance:

- Store them under `D:\20_Personal\_Private` or an equivalent protected root.
- Recommend BitLocker, VeraCrypt, or encrypted archives.
- Keep at least one offline backup for irreplaceable documents.
- Do not scatter sensitive files across Desktop, Downloads, messaging app caches, or temporary folders.
- Do not place company confidential files in personal cloud storage.
